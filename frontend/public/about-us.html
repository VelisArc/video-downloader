About SwiftDL

Our Mission

SwiftDL was created to provide a simple, fast, and free way for users to download videos from popular social media platforms. We currently focus on providing the best experience for downloading Instagram Reels and Facebook videos.

We understand that users often want to save videos for offline viewing, sharing with friends (where permitted), or creative purposes. Our goal is to make this process as easy as possible while respecting copyright laws.

Why Choose Us?

  • Fast Downloads: Our tool is optimized for speed.
  • Free & Unlimited: No hidden costs, no download limits.
  • Simple Interface: Just copy, paste, and download.
  • Privacy Focused: We don't store your URLs or download history.

Future Plans

We are constantly working to improve SwiftDL. Our team is actively developing support for more platforms, including YouTube, TikTok, Twitter, and many others. Stay tuned for updates!
